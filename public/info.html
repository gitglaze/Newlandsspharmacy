Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Newlandss Pharmacy (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and shares information about you when you visit our website, place an order or contact our support team. By using our website you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery details: the shipping and billing addresses you provide at checkout.
  • Order information: the products, quantities, order totals and payment references linked to your orders.
  • Health-related information: any details you choose to share with your order or in messages to our team, such as notes about a product.
  • Messages: the content of emails, chats and contact-form messages you send us.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, collected automatically through cookies and server logs.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, review, dispatch and deliver your orders.
  • To confirm payments and prevent fraud or misuse of our website.
  • To send order confirmations, tracking details and service messages.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To maintain, secure and improve our website and services.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to run our service: with delivery and courier partners to ship your order, with payment services to confirm your payment, with technology providers that host our website and support tools, and with authorities where the law requires it. These parties may only use your information to provide their service to us.

4. Payment information

Payments are made through the third-party payment services you choose at checkout. We do not store your wallet or account credentials. We keep a record of the order, the amount and the payment reference so that we can match your payment to your order.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies to keep your cart and checkout working, and technical cookies to understand how the website performs. Our live chat is provided by a third-party service, which may set its own cookies to run the chat. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the website may not work properly without them.

6. Data security

We use encrypted connections and access controls to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and review our safeguards regularly.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to fulfil your order, provide support, resolve disputes and meet legal and record-keeping obligations. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict certain uses, and opt out of marketing messages. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

9. Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “last updated” date above. Please review it periodically.

11. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email support@newlandsspharmacy.com or call +1 (832) 689-7495.

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about ordering, payment, delivery and managing your order.

How do I place an order?

Browse the Shop, open the product you want, choose your preferred variant (strength and pack size), then add it to your cart. When you are ready, go to Checkout, enter your delivery details and complete your payment.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, Tether (USDT) and Bitcoin. Payment instructions are shown at checkout, and your order is processed once the payment is confirmed.

Is it safe to order from your website?

Yes. Your details are sent over an encrypted connection, every order is reviewed by a registered pharmacist before it ships, and we only use your information to fulfil your order. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes. Orders shipped within the USA to a USA address receive free shipping. For deliveries outside the USA, any shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Delivery times vary depending on the supplier fulfilling your order and your delivery location. Our support team can update you on your order at any time. See Delivery Information for details.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been dispatched, we email you the tracking details so you can follow your parcel. If you have not received them, contact our support team with your order number.

Can I return my order?

No. All sales are final and we do not accept returns or exchanges, so please check your order carefully before you pay. See our Terms of Use for details.

My order arrived damaged or incorrect. What should I do?

Contact our support team with your order number and photos of the item and its packaging, and we will look into it.

Can you give me medical advice?

Our team can help with product information and your order, but we cannot replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist. Please speak to a healthcare professional before starting or changing any medicine.

How do I contact customer support?

Our team is available 24/7. Use the live chat, email support@newlandsspharmacy.com, call +1 (832) 689-7495 or message us on WhatsApp.
